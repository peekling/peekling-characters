# Licensing and attribution

Original pack tooling, tests, and documentation are licensed under Apache-2.0 in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). That license allows personal and commercial use, modification, distribution, and sublicensing subject to its terms.

Redistributions of Apache-2.0 material must comply with Section 4 of the Apache License 2.0, including its applicable notice-retention requirements. When a distribution includes a `NOTICE` file, Section 4 describes how readable copies of applicable NOTICE attributions may be provided. Apache-2.0 does not require every website using Peekling to display a visible attribution.

Each character pack is a separate package boundary with its own license metadata, full `LICENSE`, and applicable `NOTICE`. The package and character manifests must identify the same license. The archive gate verifies these records, the author, every referenced atlas, the thumbnail, and the atlas hashes recorded in `character.json`.

All 30 current official character packs use Apache-2.0. Future community or third-party packs must establish their own valid public license boundary before publication.

The Peekling name, logo, official mascot, and other distinctive brand identity remain reserved unless a specific asset license grants separate rights. See Section 6 of Apache-2.0 for the tooling license's trademark boundary.

Repository-level `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and `AUTHORS` files are the canonical records for original tooling. Generated, binary, artwork, and third-party files keep their own applicable notices instead of receiving mechanical source headers.

`AUTHORS` is a factual record for repository tooling and does not add a license condition. This summary is informational and is not legal advice.
